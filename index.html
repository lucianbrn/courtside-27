      How it works

      The shape. A best-of-seven Finals between three real championship teams — the 2008 Celtics, the 2013 Heat, the 2017 Warriors — coached through real film and real rosters. Each game is 4 quarters of 3 beats. A beat is a stretch of possessions: you coach it, the film plays it.

      Your three calls. Every beat you set an offensive play, a defensive scheme, and a tempo. Matchups matter: transition beats a press, a trap eats isolation, zones dare you to shoot.

      The chess. The opposing coach has a personality — and he adapts. Spam one play and he'll counter it. Vary your calls, hunt the counter to his counter, and the Legacy XP follows.

      Real men, real numbers. Every play is credited to a real player from those rosters. Their series stats live on the Roster screen. A few deep-bench men have no surviving era photo — their cards are name-plates, and the game says so.

      Progression. Earn XP, climb from Assistant to Dynasty Architect, and unlock the Hammer set and the Blitz on the way. Your legacy and your series are saved on this device.
